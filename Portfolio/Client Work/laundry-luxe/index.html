  • 80° for heavily soiled, durable items

3 home loads together still fit comfortably in one 18kg dry.

More on drying +

Our 18kg dryer holds the equivalent of three standard home loads, drying evenly with three heat settings.

  • High heat for durable items
  • Medium heat for everyday clothing
  • Low heat for delicate, heat-sensitive fabrics

Each load is monitored for safe, consistent drying.

Who we are

About us

Family-run, built around you

Laundry Luxe Ireland is a family-run business that puts real care into every wash and dry, making everyday life a little easier for the people who rely on us. We welcome busy parents, students, shift workers, carers, Airbnb, sports clubs, and local businesses, anyone whose laundry basket seems to multiply when they're not looking!

Gentle on skin, easy on your day

We understand that sensitive skin, allergies, and special fabric needs are part of everyday life, so our hypoallergenic detergent and gentle wash settings are designed to keep you feeling comfortable and cared for. Our machines are perfectly located in places where you can get your shopping done, grab a coffee, or enjoy a rare moment of peace, whether that's between kids' activities or while you're hiding from your own to-do list. We look after your clothes with the same care we give our own, so you can get on with the good stuff.
