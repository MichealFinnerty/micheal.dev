Bedrooms

Spacious rooms, designed as private sanctuaries.

Each generously sized private bedroom offers a true sense of home, flooded with natural light from a large window, with ample space for personal touches and a comfortable, adjustable bed. A discreet nurse call system means assistance is always just a button press away.

Every room includes complimentary Wi-Fi and Saorview digital TV, so residents stay connected with loved ones and the world.

Private en-suite bathrooms

Every room features its own accessible en-suite, with a spacious walk-in shower, grab bars, and a comfortable shower seat for a secure, refreshing experience. Non-slip flooring and ample lighting further enhance the safety of the space.

Garden

A tranquil oasis, built for every resident to explore.

Paved, wheelchair-friendly pathways wind through the garden, giving every resident the freedom to explore safely and independently. It's more than a garden, it's a place for well-being, connection, and calm.
